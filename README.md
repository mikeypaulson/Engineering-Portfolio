## Datsun 280Z Powertrain Rebuild

Rebuilding a stripped Datsun 280Z around an L28ET turbocharged engine and
Nissan 71C 5-speed transmission. The project involves drivetrain integration,
mechanical troubleshooting, fuel and cooling systems, ignition, electrical work,
and standalone engine-management integration.

### Highlights
- Removed and inspected the previous drivetrain
- Inspected and serviced engine, turbocharger, transmission, and supporting components
- Planning and integrating MegaSquirt standalone engine management
- Troubleshooting and testing components prior to final assembly

## Jeep CJ-7 Electrical System Rebuild

Rebuilt the vehicle's complete electrical system, modifying wiring, relays,
fuses, grounds, lighting, and accessories.

### Highlights
- Rebuilt and modified wiring-harness sections
- Added relays, fuses, grounds, lighting, and accessories
- Diagnosed grounding and connectivity problems using wiring diagrams and continuity testing
- Returned the electrical system to full functionality
