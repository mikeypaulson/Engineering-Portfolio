# Purdue Grand Prix Kart Racing

## Project Overview

I serve as the team lead, primary mechanic, and one of the drivers for a 20-member Purdue Grand Prix racing team operating two competition karts. My work spans mechanical setup and repair, engine and drivetrain tuning, fabrication, telemetry analysis, testing, race preparation, and team operations.

The team competes in approximately six races and events each academic year, including Purdue's Grand Prix.



## Mechanical Engineering & Race Preparation

As the primary mechanic, I am responsible for preparing and maintaining both karts throughout the season.

My work includes:

- Engine maintenance and tuning
- Clutch setup and adjustment
- Carburetor tuning
- Gearing and sprocket selection
- Chain alignment and maintenance
- Brake system service and bleeding
- Steering system maintenance
- Toe, camber, and caster adjustments
- Ride-height and chassis setup
- Axle, hub, and bearing service
- Wheel and tire setup
- Tire pressure and temperature management
- Weight distribution and corner balancing
- Electrical troubleshooting
- Fabrication, drilling, tapping, and welding

## Testing & Data Analysis

I use MyChron 5 telemetry and MYLAPS timing data alongside driver feedback to evaluate kart performance and guide setup changes.

Data from testing is used to compare:

- Lap times
- Engine RPM
- Speed
- Driver consistency
- Kart setup changes
- Mechanical issues and performance limitations

Changes are tested on track and compared against previous sessions to determine whether the setup improved performance.

## Troubleshooting & Iteration

A large part of racing involves identifying problems quickly and making changes between sessions.

For example, before a major race I diagnosed and corrected issues with the kart's braking system. After servicing and bleeding the brakes along with subsequent setup work, lap times improved by approximately one second during testing.

This process of identifying a problem, making a mechanical change, testing the kart, and analyzing the results is a major part of how I approach race preparation.

## Team Leadership

Responsibilities include:

- Organizing practices and work sessions
- Assigning mechanical and race-preparation tasks
- Coordinating equipment and parts orders
