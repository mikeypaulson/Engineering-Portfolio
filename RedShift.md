# Datsun 280Z Powertrain Rebuild

Rebuilding a stripped Datsun 280Z around an L28ET turbocharged engine and Nissan 71C 5-speed transmission. The project focuses on powertrain integration, mechanical troubleshooting, component inspection, and standalone engine-management integration.

## Project Goals
- Restore the vehicle to reliable running condition
- Integrate the L28ET turbocharged drivetrain
- Install and configure MegaSquirt engine management
- Improve serviceability and support future performance upgrades

## Initial Condition
The project began with a largely stripped chassis, no interior, and a previously installed engine and transmission that were removed to begin the rebuild.



## Drivetrain Preparation
- Removed the existing engine and transmission
- Inspected the L28ET bottom end, turbocharger, starter, alternator, distributor, intake, and supporting components
- Identified parts requiring service or replacement before installation
- Prepared the 240 mm flywheel, clutch system, and 71C 5-speed transmission for assembly

## Systems Integration
The rebuild requires coordination between the engine, transmission, fuel, cooling, ignition, electrical, and engine-management systems.

- L28ET turbocharged engine
- Nissan 71C 5-speed transmission
- N42 intake manifold
- MegaSquirt standalone ECU
- Wideband O2 system
- Updated fuel and ignition systems

## Troubleshooting & Engineering Decisions
Examples of decisions made during the rebuild:
- Evaluated engine condition before deciding not to disturb the bottom end
- Bench-tested the starter and alternator before reuse
- Checked injector resistance and identified low-impedance injectors
- Inspected turbocharger shaft play and condition
- Determined which seals, clutch components, and supporting hardware should be replaced before installation

## Current Status
The chassis and drivetrain have been disassembled and inspected, and major components are being prepared for installation. The next phase is drivetrain assembly, engine/transmission installation, fuel and electrical integration, and MegaSquirt setup.
