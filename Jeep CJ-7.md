# Jeep CJ-7 Electrical System Rebuild

## Project Overview

Over one summer, I rebuilt the complete electrical system of a Jeep CJ-7. The project involved removing the existing wiring, installing and modifying a replacement harness, adding new circuits and accessories, and troubleshooting several electrical and vehicle-system issues until the Jeep was fully functional.

## Electrical System Integration

- Rewired power distribution, ignition, lighting, and accessory systems
- Added and configured relays, fuses, grounds, connectors, and terminals
- Modified portions of the wiring harness to accommodate new lighting and accessories
- Used factory wiring diagrams and service documentation to trace and verify circuits
- Routed wiring with attention to vibration, abrasion, strain relief, and serviceability

## Troubleshooting

A major part of the project involved diagnosing issues after installation. I used wiring diagrams, continuity testing, voltage checks, and systematic circuit tracing to identify and correct:

- Grounding faults
- Connectivity issues
- Engine run-on
- Overheating-related problems
- Lighting and accessory faults

## Fabrication & Hands-On Work

The rebuild required soldering, crimping, drilling, mounting electrical components, routing harnesses, and making small modifications to accommodate the new electrical system.

## Result

The project resulted in a fully functional vehicle electrical system and gave me experience taking a large electromechanical project from disassembly and planning through installation, troubleshooting, and final validation.
